# Engagement Intelligence Brief — $PROJECT

Canonical format for the intelligence-layer synthesizer (module #4). `scripts/brief.sh
init` generates a pre-filled copy in the engagement dir at `/engage` time; this template
documents the shape. The Brief is the funnel-front prior: it allocates effort BEFORE the
expensive testing by fusing every feeder into one spend/skip/gated view.

## Synthesis (complete at /engage — the orchestrator call)

- **Spend here:** reachable lanes that hit the program's focus areas.
- **Skip / deprioritize:** gated lanes + lanes matching a low-yield negative pattern.
- **Gated (unlock to reach):** lanes that need a credential/account/device/plan we lack.

## Reachability (LANE gate — module #1)

Gated and reachable lanes from `reachability.json` (run `reachability.sh` first), plus
stack-known gates from your own notes.

## Focus areas (lead here — Rule 21)

Pulled from the intake's `## Testing Priorities`. Lead with these, not your pet thesis.
